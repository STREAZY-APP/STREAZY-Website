STREAZY – Street meet made easy

Streazy is an app that lets you spontaneously meet new people in real life. Forget endless swiping – get to know interesting people right in your area. Streazy brings "social" back to its original meaning: personal, live, and on-site. While other apps try to replace real meetings, Streazy is specifically designed to make them happen. Start a search, find people in your immediate vicinity, and make plans. Whether you're new to the city, want to meet new people, or are just looking for company – Streazy brings you together.

Start for free now and find real people near you.

Features

Group & Solo Search

The main feature of Streazy is the local search. You have the choice between Group and Solo search. If you want to get out and mingle, start a group search to join a chat group with up to 10 people nearby. If you prefer to meet just one person first, the Solo search is perfect for you.

Contact Requests & Private Chats

If you don't want to meet immediately or prefer to continue chatting privately, simply send the person a contact request. Once the other user accepts, you will be saved in each other's contact lists.

More Features with Streazy Plus

Subscribe to Streazy Plus to unlock the full range of Streazy functions. Filter your Solo search by gender and age, and expand or narrow your search radius. If you missed someone, send them a contact request directly via the "Users Nearby" section and add messages to all your contact requests.

Your Benefits

Real Meetings instead of Superficiality

Streazy was born because we were tired of superficiality, fake profiles, and money traps. How often have you felt like you didn't get a "like" just because of a profile picture that wasn't perfect? We give our users the opportunity to chat directly without pre-selection and meet spontaneously in real life. With our search, you'll truly only find people in your immediate area. This is what makes meeting up possible in the first place and reduces the chance of encountering fake accounts from the other side of the world.

Fair & Transparent

With us, there are no hidden costs or limits. Streazy can be used for free indefinitely. The number of messages and searches is also not limited. Only certain convenience features, such as filters in Solo search, are reserved for Streazy Plus subscribers. The subscription helps us avoid annoying ad pop-ups and continue developing Streazy. Our support team is dedicated to keeping Streazy free from inappropriate advertising, fake accounts, or harassment of any kind.

Safety

We take safety very seriously. Look for our photo verification (blue checkmark) and ask unverified users to verify themselves before meeting up. Use our reporting feature to inform us quickly and easily about inappropriate behavior. Always make sure to meet in public places and do not disclose sensitive data about yourself. You can find more regarding safety in chat and during meetings in our Safety Guidelines.
