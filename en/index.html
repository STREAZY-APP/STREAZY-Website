STREAZY – Street meet made easy

Streazy is an app that allows you to meet new people in real life. Forget fake accounts – meet interesting people right in your area. On Streazy, all users are verified before they can contact others. This increases the quality of the profiles and, consequently, the quality of your user experience. While other apps try to replace real-life meetings, Streazy is designed to make them happen. Start your search, find people in your immediate vicinity, and meet up. Whether you're new to the city, looking to make new friends, or just want some company – Streazy brings you together.

Get started for free now and find real people near you.

Features

Local Search & Activity Map

The main feature of STREAZY is the local search. Decide how long you want to search for and start the process. Take a look at the integrated activity map to see where other users are active near you.

Contact Requests & Private Chats

If you don't want to meet up immediately or prefer to continue the conversation privately, simply send the person a contact request. Once the other user accepts, you will be added to each other's contact lists.

Find Users Near You

Filter your local user search by gender, age, and distance. If you missed someone, send them a contact request directly via the "Users Nearby" section and add a message to your request. Use the activity map to find out what's happening in your area.

Your Benefits

Real Users and Authentic Profiles

STREAZY was born out of our frustration with fake bots, low-quality profiles, and money traps. How often have you felt contacted by fake profiles just to be lured into an overpriced subscription? We avoid such methods, and our admin team manually checks every profile to ensure a high-quality user experience.

Fair & Transparent

With us, there are no hidden costs or limits. STREAZY can be used for free without any time limits. The number of messages and searches is also not restricted. Only certain convenience features, such as specific filters or the "Users Nearby" section (which makes finding users even easier), are reserved for Streazy Plus subscribers. The subscription helps us avoid annoying pop-up ads and continue developing Streazy. Our support team is dedicated to keeping Streazy free from inappropriate advertising, fake accounts, or harassment of any kind.

Safety

We take safety very seriously. Therefore, we require photo verification from all users before they can see or contact others. Use our reporting function to quickly and easily inform us of any inappropriate behavior. Always make sure to meet in public places and do not share sensitive personal data. You can find more information regarding safety in chats and during meetups in our Safety Guidelines.
